Verify before you cite

The papers below are listed from memory of their titles, authors and venues. Search each one and confirm the details before quoting it to a professor.

Start here (this repo)

  1. /summary: the one-page write-up.
  2. docs/METHODOLOGY.md, Pillar 5: every definition.
  3. docs/DECISIONS.md, ADR-003, ADR-004, ADR-009: why the project is shaped this way.
  4. src/lib/issuance.check.ts: the claims that are tested.

Runs and fragility

  • Diamond and Dybvig, "Bank Runs, Deposit Insurance, and Liquidity", Journal of Political Economy, 1983.
  • Gorton and Zhang, "Taming Wildcat Stablecoins", University of Chicago Law Review, 2023.

How stablecoins hold their price

  • Lyons and Viswanath-Natraj, "What Keeps Stablecoins Stable?", Journal of International Money and Finance, 2023.
  • Ma, Zeng and Zhang, "Stablecoin Runs and the Centralization of Arbitrage", working paper.

Stablecoins and the traditional system

  • Liao and Caramichael, "Stablecoins: Growth Potential and Impact on Banking", Federal Reserve Board IFDP, 2022.
  • The Federal Reserve's and the Treasury Borrowing Advisory Committee's recent materials on stablecoins and Treasury demand.

Primary documents

  • The text of the GENIUS Act (S. 1582) and the current implementing rules.
  • Each issuer's reserve reports: Circle, Tether, Paxos, BlackRock's BUIDL fund documents, Ondo's OUSG and USDY pages.
  • FRED series DFEDTARU and DFF for the policy rate.

Method

  • Any event-study chapter in a standard empirical finance textbook, for abnormal returns, estimation windows and why overlapping events break independence.
  • Edward Tufte, The Visual Display of Quantitative Information, for the figure rules used here.
