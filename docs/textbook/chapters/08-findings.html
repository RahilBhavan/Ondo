What you can claim, and what you cannot

Safe to say

  • {{v:gross}} was minted and redeemed on Ethereum across five issuers, mostly USDC churn.
  • In the 2024 easing episode, the larger payment stablecoins issued more than usual and the Treasury-fund tokens less.
  • That pattern is not repeated cleanly in 2025.
  • Data are dated snapshots. Method and checks are documented.

Do not say

  • "Rate cuts cause stablecoin growth." Two episodes. No causal design.
  • "Total stablecoin issuance across all chains." The logs are Ethereum only.
  • "Nexus volumes." It is OUSG and USDY as a proxy.
  • Anything about the September 2026 rate increase. It was not analyzed.

Check yourself

Say the 2024 result in one sentence, with the caveat.

Around the 2024 cuts, USDC and USDT issuance was above their normal weeks while the Treasury-fund tokens were below, but there are only two episodes and 2025 did not repeat it.

Why is "mixed" a good word here?

It is accurate. The two episodes disagree, and it avoids implying a pattern the data cannot support.

Which of the three stablecoins is least well covered by Ethereum logs?

USDT. Tron holds about half its supply.
